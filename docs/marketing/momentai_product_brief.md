# MomentAI — Product Brief for Marketing

*A non-technical rundown for marketing AI agents. Use this as source-of-truth context when targeting audiences and creating slideshow / ad / social content.*

---

## One-liner

**MomentAI turns a photo of your moment into a custom Spotify playlist — instantly.**

Snap a picture of wherever you are — a concert, a late-night drive, a rainy coffee shop window — and MomentAI reads the mood of the image and builds a playlist that sounds like that moment feels.

## What it is

MomentAI is a web app (works like an app on your phone, no download needed) that connects photos to music. It uses AI to look at a picture and understand its atmosphere: the setting, the emotional vibe, the colors, the energy. It then translates that feeling into a fresh, personalized playlist of real Spotify tracks the user can preview immediately and save to their own Spotify account.

## How it works (from the user's point of view)

1. **Capture.** Open MomentAI and snap a photo (camera-first, BeReal-style) or upload one. Users can optionally add a short prompt to steer the vibe (e.g. "more indie", "slower songs").
2. **Get the vibe.** Within seconds, the app shows an "aesthetic profile" of the moment — the environment, the emotional mood, the color palette.
3. **Hear the moment.** A tailored playlist appears instantly. Users can preview tracks in the app, remove songs, browse extra suggestions, and add more.
4. **Keep it.** One tap saves the playlist to Spotify. Past moments live in a "Your Moments" history grid, so every photo becomes a saved memory with its own soundtrack.

**Key experience principle: no sign-up wall.** Users see their playlist *before* they're ever asked to log in or connect Spotify. Value first, account later ("3-second time-to-value").

## What makes it different

- **Instant gratification.** Photo in, playlist out, in seconds. No forms, no onboarding quiz, no login required to try it.
- **Emotion-driven, not genre-driven.** It doesn't ask "what do you like?" — it reads the *feeling* of a real moment and matches music to it.
- **Always fresh.** Playlists are generated new each time; the app actively avoids repeating songs a user has already gotten.
- **Editable, not a black box.** Users can add, remove, and swap tracks before saving.
- **Memory-keeping angle.** Every capture is stored as a "moment" — a photo + soundtrack pair — turning the app into a visual-musical diary.

## Pricing

- **Free:** 3 moments (photo-to-playlist generations) per day, forever. $0.
- **Premium:** $3.99/month — unlimited moments, no daily cap, premium unwatermarked share cards. 7-day free trial, cancel anytime, and saved playlists always stay in the user's Spotify.

## Target audiences

- **Gen Z / young millennial social sharers (roughly 16–28).** BeReal/Instagram Stories natives who photograph everyday moments and care about aesthetic and vibe. Primary audience.
- **Spotify power users & playlist curators.** People who make playlists for every mood, trip, and season — MomentAI automates and inspires their curation.
- **Concert-goers, travelers, and night-drive romantics.** Anyone in "main character" moments who wants a soundtrack to match — festivals, road trips, city nights, golden-hour walks.
- **Aesthetic/mood communities.** Audiences into moodboards, Pinterest, "-core" aesthetics (cottagecore, city pop, dark academia), lo-fi study scenes.
- **Journalers and memory-keepers.** People who like capturing and revisiting life moments; the photo + soundtrack history is a new kind of diary.

## Messaging angles & hooks (for slides, ads, social)

- "Your life has a soundtrack. We just found it."
- "Snap a photo. Hear the moment."
- "From picture to playlist in seconds — no sign-up, no quiz."
- "Stop searching for the right playlist. Show us where you are."
- "Every moment, scored." (used in-app: "Every moment you catch, scored.")
- Before/after storytelling: rainy window photo → soft indie playlist; festival crowd photo → high-energy set.
- FOMO-free trial framing: try it free 3 times a day, forever; unlimited for less than a coffee ($3.99/mo, 7 days free).

## Tone & brand feel

Dark-mode, minimal, slightly cinematic. Feels like BeReal meets Spotify: spontaneous, aesthetic, personal. Copy should be short, lowercase-casual where appropriate, emotionally evocative rather than feature-listy. Lead with the *feeling* (the moment, the memory, the vibe), not the technology. Mention "AI" as the magic behind it, not the headline.

## Things to avoid in marketing copy

- Don't promise offline use or a native App Store app (it's a web app).
- Don't say it plays full songs in-app — in-app playback is previews; full listening happens in Spotify.
- Don't position it as a Spotify replacement — it's a companion that saves playlists *to* Spotify.
- Avoid technical jargon (models, APIs, databases). The audience buys the feeling, not the stack.
